The one that was missing.

18 December 2022 · Lusail Stadium

Argentina 3–3 France
(4–2 on penalties)

Messi scored twice in the final — once from the spot, once in extra time — and converted his penalty in the shootout. Seven goals and three assists across the tournament. The Golden Ball for the second time, the first player ever to win it twice. Then, a year later, the eighth Ballon d'Or.

The world watches

More than a footballer.

From Rosario to every corner of the earth.
